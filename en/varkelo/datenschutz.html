Privacy policy

Varkelo: learn Greek · As of November 2026 · App version 0.4.6

In short

Varkelo is a learning app that runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no advertising.

The app does not need the internet for learning: words, pictures, audio, stories and grammar are all inside the app. It needs a connection for one thing only – to buy or check the “Varkelo Premium” subscription through Google Play. Everything you learn stays with you.

What is stored on your device

So that the app remembers where you are, it stores the following on your device only:

  • which words you have already learned and when they are due again
  • your favourites
  • coins, badges and the number of days you have learned
  • your settings: daily goal, colour theme, sound, “My path” on or off
  • whether your subscription is active – just “yes” or “no”, nothing more

This information never leaves your device. It is not analysed, not transmitted and not combined with other data.

The “Varkelo Premium” subscription

Stories and grammar from level B1 are available with a subscription for €4.99 per month, cancellable monthly. Everything else – the whole vocabulary, the alphabet, learning to read, all games and levels A1 and A2 – stays free.

The purchase is handled by Google Play: payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds run through your Google account, not through us. We never learn your name or your payment details. The app only asks Google whether the signed-in Google account has an active subscription and remembers the answer (“yes” or “no”) on the device.

Google's privacy policy applies to this processing: https://policies.google.com/privacy

No sharing with third parties

This app has no advertising, no analytics tools, no crash reporting and no social media buttons. No data is sent to us or to advertising networks.

The only outside connection is to Google Play – when you buy or restore the subscription, or when the app checks at start-up whether it is still active. Pictures and audio recordings are entirely inside the app and are never downloaded.

Permissions

The app has exactly one permission: internet. It is used only for the subscription through Google Play. The app does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

How long data is kept

The information stored on your device remains until you reset it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes it from the device.

Your rights

As we do not collect any personal data, there is nothing we hold about you that we could report. Regardless of this, under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Children and young people

Varkelo is meant for anyone who wants to learn Greek and its content is suitable for all ages. Since the app collects no data at all, it poses no risks for younger users either.

Changes to this policy

Should the app ever do anything beyond what it does today, this policy will be updated beforehand. The current version can always be found in the app as well (Settings → Datenschutz).
