Varkelo

Learn Greek – with pictures, audio and the Greek alphabet from day one.

▶ Coming soon to Google Play 🇩🇪 The app is made for German speakers – all explanations are in German.

Who it is for

For German-speaking beginners who really want to learn Greek – for their holidays, for their family, or because the country simply won't let them go.

Free – forever

  • The entire vocabulary: 5,465 words in 16 topics, each with a picture and an audio recording
  • The alphabet and “Learning to read Greek” in 11 steps
  • All games, smart review, favourites and your learning path
  • 24 Greek proverbs and what they mean
  • Stories and grammar for levels A1 and A2 – 32 stories, 37 lessons

Varkelo Premium

€4.99 per month, incl. VAT

  • 64 more stories for levels B1 to C2 – tap any word, with a cultural tip and questions
  • 54 more grammar lessons for levels B1 to C2

How the subscription works

  • It renews automatically every month until you cancel.
  • Cancel any time in a few clicks – go straight to your subscriptions on Google Play (or in the Play Store: profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). The month you paid for runs to its end; after that you pay nothing more.
  • After cancelling, levels B1 to C2 are locked again. The free part – the entire vocabulary, A1 and A2 – stays, and your progress stays saved.
  • Payment goes through Google Play. Google shows the exact price for your country before you buy.
